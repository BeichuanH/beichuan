I am a Battery CAE Engineer at Volvo Cars, working with battery-cell analysis and modelling for automotive applications. My technical interests lie in the intersection of physical modelling, system identification, simulation, estimation, and optimization.

Before joining Volvo Cars, I worked as an engineering consultant at AFRY. I received my Ph.D. in Machine Design from KTH Royal Institute of Technology in 2023, where my doctoral research focused on energy and exergy analysis of combustion and gas-exchange systems. I also worked on battery modelling, mechatronic systems, and hybrid-powertrain optimization at the KTH Unit of Mechatronics.

Earlier, at the KTH Transport Planning Division, I studied optimal construction operations. That work involved motion planning for autonomous construction machines, data-driven emission models, and discrete-event simulation. Before moving to Sweden, I studied energy and power engineering at Wuhan University of Technology and worked at the Cummins East Asia R&D Center.

Research Interests
======

My work has covered several application areas, but the underlying questions have remained similar: how to build useful models from physical understanding and measured data, how to identify the quantities that matter, and how to use those models for engineering decisions.

Current and previous interests include:

* lithium-ion battery modelling and parameter estimation;
* energy management and optimization of electrified powertrains;
* system identification, state estimation, and model-based control;
* energy and exergy analysis of thermal systems;
* combustion and pulsating exhaust-flow measurement;
* simulation and optimization of transport and construction operations.

Background
======

My academic research was carried out mainly at the [KTH Unit of Mechatronics](https://www.kth.se/mmk/mechatronics), the KTH Internal Combustion Engine research environment, and the Competence Center for Gas Exchange (CCGEx). It included experimental work, physical and data-driven modelling, numerical simulation, and collaboration with industrial partners in the automotive, marine, and construction sectors.
